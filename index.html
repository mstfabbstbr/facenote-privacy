FaceNote Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2025

Introduction

FaceNote respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the FaceNote app.

Data Collection

FaceNote does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app only requires access to your device's camera and microphone to record video messages. These permissions are used exclusively within the app for the purpose of creating and sending video messages.

Network & Services

FaceNote does not connect to any external servers, networks, or third-party services. All recordings remain on your device and are not transmitted or stored elsewhere.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or distribute any of your data or recordings with third parties.

User Rights

You have full control over FaceNote's access to your camera and microphone. You can revoke permissions at any time via your device settings.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@facenote.app

Thank you for using FaceNote!
